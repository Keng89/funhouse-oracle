♿ Accessibility

Last updated October 29, 2026

We want everyone to enjoy Funhouse Oracle.

  • Every character's words are shown as text as well as spoken. Captions can be turned on with the CC button.
  • Text size can be increased in Settings → Text size.
  • Slower speech and Beginner support help learners and people who need more time.
  • The App respects your device's "reduce motion" setting for animations.
  • Most buttons have labels for screen readers, and most of the App works with a keyboard.

If something is hard to use, tell us at paidigitalstore@gmail.com and we'll work on it.
