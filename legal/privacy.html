🔒 Privacy Policy

Last updated October 29, 2026

Funhouse Oracle is designed so that your conversations, memories and progress stay on your device. This policy explains what happens to your information. It is written to meet Canada's PIPEDA and Quebec's Law 25, and to respect the GDPR (EU/UK), US state privacy laws such as the CCPA, and the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

Who is responsible

Pai Keng Kuan, operator of Funhouse Oracle, is responsible for personal information under this policy and is the person in charge of the protection of personal information. Contact: paidigitalstore@gmail.com.

What we collect on our servers

Nothing that identifies you. The App has no account sign-up, no analytics, no advertising trackers and no cookies. We don't sell or rent personal information, and we don't share it for advertising.

What stays on your device

The App saves these items in your browser's local storage on your device, only so the App works for you:

  • your name or nickname (if you give one), language and settings;
  • your chats, saved conversations and the memories your companion keeps;
  • learning progress, flashcards, goals, to-dos, journal entries and mood check-ins;
  • Kids profiles, a scrambled (hashed) parent PIN, and kids' usage minutes;
  • characters, photos or 3D models you add (kept in the browser's database on your device).

You can view and delete memories in Settings → Memory, and delete everything in Settings → Legal & privacy → Delete all my data. Uninstalling the App or clearing your browser's site data also deletes it. Because we never receive this information, we cannot see it, recover it, or delete it for you. Use Settings → Backup to keep a copy.

Services your device connects to

To work, the App loads files from these services. They can see your device's IP address and basic browser information, like any website you visit:

  • GitHub Pages (hosting the App) and claude.ai (if you open the App there);
  • Google Fonts (letter styles);
  • jsDelivr and Hugging Face (only when you choose to download an in-browser AI model or the natural voice);
  • the AI or voice provider you connect with your own key. When connected, your messages and the context needed to reply (such as recent chat lines and saved memories) are sent from your device to that provider and handled under their privacy policy. You can switch back to the offline brain at any time.

Microphone and speech

When you tap a microphone button, your browser's built-in speech recognition turns your voice into text. Some browsers (for example Chrome) send that audio to the browser maker's servers to do this, under the browser maker's privacy policy. The App itself does not record or store your voice. Spoken replies use your device's voices, the offline natural voice, or the voice provider you connect. In Kids profiles the microphone is off by default.

Children

  • The main App is for people aged 13 and over.
  • Children use the App through Kids profiles that a parent creates with a parent PIN.
  • In Kids profiles: chats are not saved, the companion's memory is turned off, cloud AI is not used, stories are made offline, there are no ads and no purchases, and the microphone stays off unless the parent turns it on.
  • We don't collect children's personal information on our servers. A child's name, avatar and progress stay on the family's device, and a parent can delete a Kids profile at any time in Settings → Kids profiles.
  • If you believe a child's information has reached us, contact us and we will delete it.

How long information is kept

Information on your device stays until you delete it. Kids' daily usage records are kept for 14 days and then removed automatically. We keep emails you send us only as long as needed to answer you, and no longer than 2 years.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or move your personal information, to withdraw consent, and to complain to a privacy regulator (in Canada, the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada; in Quebec, the Commission d'accès à l'information). Because your data lives on your device, you can do most of this yourself in Settings. For anything else, email paidigitalstore@gmail.com. We answer within 30 days.

Security

Your data never leaves your device unless you connect an outside AI or voice service. Keep your device locked and your browser updated. API keys you enter are stored only in your browser. Never share them.

International users

If you connect an outside AI provider, your messages may be processed in other countries by that provider.

Changes

We will update this page and its date when practices change, and the App will ask you to review important changes.
