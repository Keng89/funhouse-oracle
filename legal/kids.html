🧸 Kids & Parents

Last updated October 29, 2026

Funhouse Oracle's main App is for ages 13 and up. Children can use it only through a Kids profile created by a parent or guardian.

How Kids profiles protect children

  • Parent PIN: only a grown-up with the PIN can leave kids mode, add time, or change settings. If the PIN is forgotten, it can be reset after a grown-up maths question.
  • Only kid-friendly areas: stories, bedtime stories, story maker, character games, English for young learners and a gentle chat friend. The Companion, coaching, fortunes, wishes, AI setup and wardrobe are hidden.
  • Offline and private: chats in kids mode use the offline brain and are not saved. Nothing about your child is remembered. No cloud AI is used, stories are made offline, and there are no ads or purchases.
  • Microphone off by default: you can turn it on per child. Speech recognition may be processed by your browser's maker (see the Privacy Policy).
  • Daily time limits and a friendly "time's up" screen.
  • AI reminders: the character regularly reminds your child that it is an AI, not a real person, and suggests breaks.
  • Separate progress: each child has their own English progress on your device, and you can delete a profile at any time.

For parents

We recommend staying nearby while young children use the App, and talking with them about AI: it can be fun and helpful, but it isn't a person and it can make mistakes. If your child shares something worrying, the App guides them to tell a trusted grown-up and shows kids' helplines.
