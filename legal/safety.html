🛟 AI & Safety

Last updated October 29, 2026

You are talking to an AI

Every character in Funhouse Oracle is an artificial intelligence, not a human. The App shows this when you first start, on the stage label ("AI"), and at the top of every chat. Characters can sound warm and remember things you share, but they don't have feelings and they can be wrong. The Companion and AI chat may not be suitable for some minors. Children should use a Kids profile.

Our crisis and self-harm protocol

We take messages about suicide and self-harm seriously, whichever AI brain is switched on:

  • Detection: before any reply, the App checks your message on your device for words and phrases about suicide, self-harm or wanting to die, in English and Vietnamese.
  • Response: the App immediately shows a support card with crisis lines, and the character answers with care: it encourages you to reach out to a crisis line, emergency services or someone you trust, and it does not give information about methods.
  • No harmful content: the App's instructions to every AI brain tell it never to encourage, describe or give instructions for suicide or self-harm, and to move to support instead.
  • Kids profiles: children are guided to talk to a trusted grown-up right away and are shown kids' helplines.
  • Privacy: this check happens on your device. Nothing is reported to us or anyone else.

Crisis lines

If you are in immediate danger, call your local emergency number (911 in Canada and the US, 112 in Europe, 999 in the UK, 115 or 113 in Vietnam).

  • Canada and the US: call or text 988 (Suicide Crisis Helpline / Suicide & Crisis Lifeline).
  • Kids and teens in Canada: Kids Help Phone, call 1-800-668-6868 or text CONNECT to 686868.
  • UK and Ireland: Samaritans, call 116 123.
  • Australia: Lifeline, call 13 11 14.
  • Vietnam: child protection hotline 111. Medical emergencies 115.
  • Anywhere else: find a free, confidential helpline at findahelpline.com.

Healthy use

  • The App reminds you to take a break after long chats, and reminds children regularly that they are talking to an AI.
  • Kids profiles have daily time limits set by a parent.
  • The Companion is there to support you, not to replace friends, family or professional help.

Content rules

The App is built for wholesome fun and learning. It is designed not to produce sexual content, and never sexual content in a Kids profile. If you connect an outside AI provider, that provider's own safety rules also apply. If you see something harmful, email paidigitalstore@gmail.com with what happened so we can improve.

Photos and real people

You can make a photo talk. Only use photos you own or have permission to use, and never use them to deceive, embarrass or harm anyone.
